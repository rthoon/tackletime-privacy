Privacy Policy for TackleTime

Last updated: July 8, 2026

TackleTime ("the app") is a personal alarm, medication reminder, calendar, grocery list, and task app for Android. This policy explains what data the app collects, how it's used, and how it's stored.

Summary

TackleTime is built to keep your data on your device by default. Nothing is collected for advertising, nothing is sold, and there is no TackleTime account or company server that your personal app data is sent to. Data may reach Google services when you connect Google Calendar, consent to optional Gemini AI features, or when the app automatically sends limited crash diagnostics through Firebase Crashlytics.

Data We Collect and How It's Used

Medication and health information

Medication names, doses, schedules, and notes you enter are stored on your device and are never uploaded to any server we operate — there isn't one. This data powers reminders, adherence tracking, and refill alerts entirely on-device. Some optional AI features (medication label/list scanning, and calendar/medication summaries like "Plan My Day") may send medication data to Google's Gemini AI, described in "Photos" and "AI features" below; all are off unless you've given consent, and none are required to use the app.

Calendar and account information

If you choose to sign in with Google, TackleTime requests read-only access to your Google Calendar so it can display your events and let you set alarms for them. We also receive your name, email, and profile photo from Google Sign-In to show you're signed in. This information is used only within the app; it is not shared with any third party and is not stored on any server other than Google's own, which you already control through your Google Account.

Location

If you set up a grocery store location, the app stores that location on your device to detect when you're nearby (geofencing) and show a reminder notification. Location is checked only while the app is in use unless you explicitly turn on "Background Alerts" for a store, which is off by default. Your location is never transmitted anywhere — it's compared to your saved store locations entirely on your device.

Photos (medication labels, medication lists, grocery lists, recipes)

When you use a photo-scan feature — medication label/list scanning, grocery list scanning, or recipe scanning — the photo is processed to extract information. TackleTime tries on-device processing first, which never leaves your phone. If your device doesn't support on-device AI, and you've given consent (see below), the photo may be sent to Google's Gemini AI cloud service to extract the same information. The photo itself is not stored by TackleTime after processing. Only the extracted result (e.g. a medication draft, a grocery item list) is kept, and nothing else about the photo is retained.

AI features that generate summaries or suggestions

Several optional features send text from your own data — calendar events, medication names/notes, task titles, or grocery items — to Google's Gemini AI to generate a summary, checklist, or suggestion: "Plan My Day" and "Events Briefing" (calendar/medication/grocery/task summaries), "Break Down Task" (task-to-subtask suggestions), "Smart List" and "AI Edit" (grocery list suggestions/edits), and "Quick Add" (turning a typed sentence into an alarm or task). All of these are subject to the same on-device-first, consent-gated process as photo scans — nothing is sent unless you've given AI consent, and each feature has a manual, non-AI way to do the same thing (typing the alarm/task in directly, editing the grocery list by hand, etc.) if you'd rather not use it.

AI consent

Before any photo or text is ever sent to a cloud AI service, TackleTime asks for your explicit permission. You can decline and use on-device text recognition (OCR) or manual entry instead, or change this choice at any time under More → Notifications.

Crash diagnostics

TackleTime uses Google Firebase Crashlytics to identify crashes, application-not-responding errors, and certain non-fatal technical failures so we can improve stability and alarm reliability. Crashlytics runs automatically and may send crash stack traces, relevant application state at the time of a failure, device and app metadata, and a randomly generated Crashlytics installation identifier to Google. TackleTime does not intentionally attach your name, email address, medication list, calendar contents, grocery lists, tasks, photos, or precise location to crash reports.

Crashlytics data is used only for diagnosing and fixing technical problems, not for advertising or marketing. Google states that Crashlytics crash data and associated identifiers are retained for 90 days before removal begins. More information is available in Firebase's privacy and security documentation.

Sharing a list or backup with someone else

The Grocery tab lets you share a single list, and More → Backup & Restore lets you export all of your app data (alarms, medications, calendar rules, grocery lists, tasks, and settings) as a block of text. Both are entirely under your control: nothing is sent anywhere until you tap Share and choose where it goes through your device's own Share Sheet (e.g. Messages, email, Drive). TackleTime does not have a server to send this data to, and does not see or store a copy of it.

Data Storage

All app data (alarms, medications, calendar cache, grocery lists, tasks, settings) is stored locally on your device. It is not backed up to any cloud service by the app itself, and Android's own automatic cloud backup is disabled for this app's data.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties for advertising or marketing purposes. The only data that ever leaves your device is:

  • Calendar reads and Google Sign-In authentication, sent directly to Google's own APIs under your Google Account.
  • Photos or text (from your calendar, medications, groceries, or tasks) sent to Google's Gemini AI API, only when on-device processing isn't available and only after you've given consent — see "Photos" and "AI features" above for the full list of what this covers.
  • Limited crash and diagnostic information sent automatically to Google Firebase Crashlytics to identify and fix crashes, application-not-responding errors, and non-fatal technical failures.
  • Anything you explicitly export or share (a grocery list, a full backup), only when you tap Share yourself and only to wherever you choose to send it.

Your Choices

  • You can decline AI cloud processing at any time and use on-device OCR instead.
  • You can turn off Background Alerts for grocery reminders at any time (off by default).
  • You can sign out of Google at any time, which removes cached calendar data and account info from the app.
  • You can delete all app data by uninstalling the app, or by using the in-app Backup & Restore screen to export/clear your data.

Children's Privacy

TackleTime is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect data from children.
